About us

Rooted in craft, grown with care.

R&S Gardening began as a family business with a simple belief: every garden, however big or small, deserves to be loved. Today we bring the same personal care to every project. We design, build and nurture spaces that come alive through every season.

Although we've grown since then, our values have stayed the same. We take care of building your whole garden and complete it with wooden garden houses, gazebos or terraces that will bring you joy and comfort for many years. We believe in a friendly, fair approach, which is why our customers love coming back.
